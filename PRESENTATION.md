**Speaker:**

## 2:00 - 3:30 | LIVE DEMO Part 1: Privacy & "Fake Keys"
**Speaker:**  
*(Navigate to 'Submit Report' page)*
"But wait—if I report a cartel, they might retaliate against my wallet, right?
**Not with SecureTransac.**

*Show the 'Stealth Identity' feature.*
We implemented **Fake Client IDs** (Stealth Keys).
Watch this. I want to report a suspicious contract. But instead of using my main wallet, I click **'Generate Stealth Identity'**.
The system generates a cryptographically valid, ephemeral 'Fake Key'. I sign the report with *this* key. The network verifies the report is valid but has **zero way** to trace it back to me. **Whistleblowers are 100% protected.**"
